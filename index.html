About Me

I build industrial automation that is reliable in real production, not only on paper.

My Story

During my diploma studies, I attended a workshop where I saw something that immediately clicked for me. On a screen, there was a ladder-like diagram, and that diagram was controlling real robots. It looked simple, but the impact was huge. I still remember thinking, if a few lines of logic can move a robot, then how cool will it be to see it controlling big machines in a factory.

That moment pushed me to take a course in automation, and soon after I got my 1st job and started working in the industry. In the beginning I was learning by doing, understanding sensors, electrical wiring, signals, and why machines fail when one small condition is wrong. Step by step, projects became bigger, and the problems became more real.

Within two years, I reached a point where I was not only writing the logic, but also guiding other engineers working under and alongside me. We worked shoulder to shoulder, programming systems, testing them, and commissioning complex automation for major customers. That journey shaped how I work today: structured, calm under pressure, and always focused on what will actually work on the shopfloor.

What I Do Today

I have 3 years of experience in industrial automation and commissioning, working across sectors such as automotive manufacturing, intralogistics and material handling, food processing, pharma production, and utility and process industries. My work covers planning, development, site commissioning, troubleshooting, customer coordination, and handover documentation, so systems stay stable not only during trials but also after go-live.

After delivering projects for customers across different countries and working in fast-paced shopfloor environments, I moved to Germany to pursue my master’s in digital engineering. I enjoy combining real industrial experience with modern digital skills, and I am building my future around smarter, more connected automation systems.
